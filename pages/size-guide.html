Find your perfect fit for the perfect rest.

How to Measure

For the most accurate results, measure yourself in your undergarments using a soft measuring tape.

Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest.

Waist: Measure around your natural waistline.

Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
