Garment Care

Make your favorites last a lifetime with mindful care.

The Golden Rule

Organic cotton is a living fiber. To maintain its natural softness and strength, treat it with kindness. Less is more when it comes to washing.

How to Wash

  • Machine Wash: Use a gentle cycle with cold water (30°C max).
  • Detergent: Use mild, eco-friendly detergents. Avoid bleach or harsh stain removers.
  • Colors: Wash with similar colors to prevent any minor bleeding.

Drying & Ironing

  • Drying: Air dry flat in the shade. Tumble drying on high heat may cause shrinkage.
  • Ironing: Use a low-medium heat setting while the garment is slightly damp for best results.

Pro Longevity Tips

Steam instead of Iron

Steaming is gentler on the fibers and keeps them fluffy.

Store with Lavender

Keep your sleepwear fresh and protect from moths naturally.
