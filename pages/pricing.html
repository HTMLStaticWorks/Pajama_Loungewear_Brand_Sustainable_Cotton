Common Questions

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes, you can pause or skip a month anytime through your account dashboard. No commitments, just comfort.

Are the sets sustainable?

Every piece in our subscription plans is made from GOTS-certified organic cotton or OEKO-TEX silk, ensuring ethical production from start to finish.

What if the size doesn't fit?

Members enjoy free returns and exchanges on all subscription items. Simply use the pre-paid label included in your box.
